# User guide

## Sign in

Open the local app and enter the administrator username and password configured during setup. Use Sign out in the lower left when finished.

## Dashboard

The overview shows counts from the asset database, the current status distribution, the last week of verification activity, recent assets and latest checks.

## Add or edit an asset

Choose **Add asset**, fill the required fields and save. The application assigns an ID and makes its QR image. Open an asset record and choose **Edit** to update its details. Serial numbers must be unique.

## Download or print a QR label

Open the asset details page. **Download** saves a PNG QR code. **Print** opens a simple label page; select Print label and print it at a suitable size, then attach it to the physical item.

## Scan and verify

Open **Scan & verify**, choose Start camera and allow camera permission. Hold a label steady inside the camera view. If camera access is unavailable, enter the `AST-` ID in the manual lookup box. Review the asset location and serial number, choose a verification result, add optional remarks and save. The result changes the asset's current status.

## Search and filter assets

The Assets page searches asset ID, name, serial number, location and department. Status, category and department filters can be combined. The page shows ten rows at a time; use the page controls to move through results.

## Verification history

Open **Verification history** to search saved checks by asset, verifier or remarks, or filter by result. Open an asset name to see its detail page and latest checks.

## Delete an asset

Use Delete asset on its detail page and confirm. This permanently removes the asset and its related verification records.
